Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Shelby Twp Plumbing (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website at shelbytwpplumbing.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or estimate request form on our website, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, and a description of the services you need. We do not collect any information automatically beyond what standard web hosting logs may record (such as IP address and browser type).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry and provide you with a free estimate or answer your questions. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact Forms

Our contact forms are processed by a third-party form service. Submissions are sent to us via email and are subject to that service’s own privacy practices. We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics software. We do not use cookies to identify you or track your behavior across websites.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by phone at 586-496-4678 or by using the contact form on our home page.
